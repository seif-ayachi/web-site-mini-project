WELCOME TO CAP BON

Who are we?

Cap Bon Explorer invites you to discover the heritage, know-how and landscapes of the Cap Bon region. From the lively streets of Nabeul to the coast of Kélibia and the historic site of Kerkouane, our peninsula offers something special around every corner.

Our project brings together local experiences that celebrate nature, culture and craftsmanship. Browse the activities, explore the events diary and use our reservation simulator to prepare a visit. This website is an educational project, and all booking details are simulated.
